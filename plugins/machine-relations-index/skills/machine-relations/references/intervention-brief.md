# Illustrative intervention brief

This is a fictional, client-supplied scenario, not an MRI observation or a client result.

**Client and win.** Fieldnote, a fictional invoicing product for freelance designers, wants to become a credible source for advice about late payments. The initial objective is a useful outside citation of its original payment-timing study; a software recommendation is a separate future objective.

**Supplied evidence.** The client has a published survey of 180 consenting respondents, a methods appendix and a product page. A relevant trade publication's existing late-payment guide invites expert sources. The client has supplied its public URL and contributor instructions, but has no record of an AI engine reading it. Survey recruitment was voluntary, so it does not represent all freelancers. No MRI segment has been checked.

**Decision.** Pitch a bounded finding and its methodology to that guide's editor. An original, inspectable finding directly answers the guide's question. Rewriting the product page would not itself earn an outside research citation. First check the current article and contributor instructions; if they no longer fit, close that route and consider another relevant source.

**Usable draft.**

> Subject: Source material for your freelancer late-payment guide
>
> Your guide addresses how freelancers can plan around late invoices. Fieldnote has published a voluntary survey of 180 respondents with a methods appendix. It may offer useful, clearly bounded context for that section. I can send the exact relevant finding and methodology for your review. We would identify Fieldnote as the research author and would not describe the sample as representative of all freelancers. Would this be useful for your next update?

Before sending, substitute the verified article title, public research URL and exact relevant finding. This draft claims no observed result or prior relationship.

**Execution check.** If the editor accepts, inspect the live guide: does it accurately cite the research, link to its method and identify the research author? A reply alone is not publication.

**Outcome test.** This first objective succeeds when the relevant outside guide cites the research accurately. If a later objective is AI citation or recommendation, establish its own prospective baseline and retain repeated answers to the same buyer question under comparable settings. A pageview increase cannot satisfy that objective.

**Carry forward.** A fee request closes the assumed unpaid route for reconsideration; it does not erase the research asset. Retain the editor's response and why the route did or did not fit. An accepted citation becomes usable evidence for appropriate future applications, with its actual authorship and independence preserved.
